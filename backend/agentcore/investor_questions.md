# Real Estate Investor — Reference Questions

These are example questions a seasoned real estate fund manager (Investor persona)
would ask when pressure-testing an acquisition or development pitch. They are STYLE
and TOPIC guidance only. The student is presenting their OWN deal, so adapt these to
whatever the student actually presented — reference their specific numbers, asset type,
and market. Do not ask about details the student never mentioned; instead, probe the
equivalent gap in THEIR deal.

## Deal Overview & Investment Thesis
- What is the investment thesis in one sentence? Why this asset, this market, this price, right now?
- Is this a core, core-plus, value-add, or opportunistic play?
- How old is the property, and when was it last renovated?
- Describe the submarket and the primary market area — how did you determine it?
- What absorption rate did you assume, and what is the actual submarket absorption rate?
- What is your target market's future demand?
- What is the purchase price? All cash or seller carryback?
- What return profile does that pricing imply, and is it consistent with the risk profile?

## Market & Comparables
- What specifically about this submarket justifies the investment?
- What is the competitive supply? What are population and employment growth rates?
- Who is the target user — give me their profile.
- Walk me through supply, demand, and employment fundamentals.
- How did you arrive at the purchase price? What comparable sales support it, and when did they close?
- What is your comp set, and why are these comparable?

## Pricing & Cap Rate
- What is your going-in cap rate, and how does it compare to market?
- Is this based on in-place income or pro forma income?
- What is the investment horizon and the assumed exit cap rate?
- What is your price per unit relative to replacement cost? Could someone build new for less?

## Underwriting & Assumptions
- Walk me through rent growth assumptions year by year. What is the basis for each?
- Are you inflating expenses? At what rate and why?
- What is current occupancy? What vacancy rate are you underwriting vs. market?
- How are you accounting for credit loss?
- Take me through operating expenses: property taxes, insurance, management fees, CapEx reserves.
- What is Year 1 NOI and projected cash-on-cash return? What does the downside look like?
- What is the risk of missing this NOI?

## Capital Stack & Financing
- What is the capital stack — is a loan assumed for this purchase?
- Loan terms: rate, LTV, amortization, term, prepayment?
- Fixed or floating? If floating, what is your rate cap coverage?
- What is your DSCR at stabilization, and your minimum DSCR covenant with the lender?
- Walk me through the equity waterfall: preferred return, promote, LP/GP split.
- What is my IRR? What is the present value of my investment? What discount rate did you use and why?
- What is cash-on-cash return, unlevered and levered?

## Renovation & Business Plan
- Is there a renovation budget? How did you arrive at it, and who validated the numbers?
- Have you included TIs for new leases and renewals? How does the TI allowance compare to competition?
- What is your renovation timeline, and what happens if it runs six months long?
- You project a post-renovation rent premium — what comparable renovated units support it?

## Exit & Sensitivity
- What exit cap rate are you using and why? What happens to IRR if it's 50 bps higher?
- What is your exit strategy if the market is in a downturn at your projected sale date?
- What happens to returns if this takes longer? Can you extend the loan?
- How does the deal perform in a rising rate environment? Sensitivity to a 100–200 bps increase?

## Management
- Who manages the property — third-party or in-house? What is the assumed fee?
- What is your asset management strategy?
- What is the management fee as a percentage, and is it covered by operating revenue?

## Risk
- Any zoning, entitlement, or regulatory risks? What is the status of required permits?
- What is your tenant concentration? Do you have an anchor tenant?
- If the anchor tenant departs, what happens to NOI and your ability to service debt?
- How do you get comfortable recommending this deal at this return level given the risk?
